# DEADLINE-REMINDER-CRON — Product Owner Path

**Applicable Policy IDs:** DOC-005, SDLC-001

## Inputs and preconditions

- Approved reminder rule: open tasks due from 7 days ago through tomorrow; daily at 08:05 WIB.

## Actions and decisions

- Confirms after release that reminders arrive for assignees and reporters and that old overdue tasks are no longer reminded.
- Requests a schedule or window change through a new decision (ADR-040 records the current rule).

## Outputs and handoff

- Release decision in [release.md](../release.md) §3.
